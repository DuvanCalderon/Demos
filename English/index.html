STORY 01 · EVERYDAY ENGLISH

The Missing Bouquet

A wedding surprise, a mischievous dog, and a friendship worth celebrating.

First, listen without reading. Then reveal the transcript and listen again. Finally, answer the questions.

Ready to listen. Press Play story.

Vocabulary in context

On Saturday morning, Emma woke up with bleary eyes. She had stayed up late helping her best friend, Lily, get ready for her wedding.

At the wedding venue, the bride looked worried. “My bouquet is gone!” she said. Emma checked every room, but the flowers were nowhere to be found. Then she noticed a small bush moving outside the window.

Behind the bush was Max, Lily’s dog, sitting next to the missing bouquet. He had taken it from a table and hidden it in the garden. Emma ran outside. When she bent down to pick up the flowers, Max jumped on her back. She fell to the ground and started laughing.

When Emma returned with the bouquet, Lily began to blush. “I thought the wedding was ruined,” she said. Emma smiled. “A little adventure makes a better story.”

Later, Max sat quietly at the bottom of the stairs while everyone celebrated. Emma did not boast about saving the day. She simply felt happy about the strong bond she shared with her friend.

Tip: Listen once with the transcript hidden. Try to understand the situation rather than every single word.
