Quick guide

Three forms. One verb.

am

I am ready.
I'm ready.
Am I ready?

is

She is ready.
She isn't ready.
Is she ready?

are

They are ready.
They aren't ready.
Are they ready?

Negative: add not after be. Question: move be before the subject. Positive short answer: use the full verb, e.g. Yes, I am. Negative short answers may use contractions.
