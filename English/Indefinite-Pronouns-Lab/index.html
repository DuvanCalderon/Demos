Change the situation and observe the wording. A question can use some- when you expect a yes or make an offer.

One negative is enough

Standard English: I found nothing. / I didn't find anything. Avoid I didn't find nothing when you mean zero.

Singular verb, even for a group

Everyone is ready. Nobody knows. Someone left their bag. Singular they is natural when gender is unknown.

Spelling matters

No one is two words (no-one is also used, especially in British English). Nobody, nothing and nowhere are single words. Every one is different from everyone.

Adjectives come after the word

Something useful, someone friendly, somewhere quiet. Not useful something.
